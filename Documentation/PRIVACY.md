## What Crony stores on your computer

- **Settings**: volume, display, accessibility and chat preferences, your chosen player name and avatar.
- **Safety record**: moderation decisions about your own chat (warnings, pauses), players you blocked, and your appeals.
- **Problem log** (`crony-errors.log`): error messages from the game, kept so you can choose to send them with a
  bug report. It is never uploaded automatically.

You can clear settings with Settings > Reset to defaults, or delete the game's data folder.

## What happens when you play online

- **Online rooms** use Unity Multiplayer Services (Relay and Lobby). To join a room, the game signs you in
  anonymously with Unity Authentication, which gives your device a random player ID. No email or password is asked
  for. Unity processes this as described in the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Same Wi-Fi play** connects directly to the host's computer on your local network.
- **Text chat** messages travel through the host's computer to the other players in the match. The host's game
  checks them against the community rules. Messages are not saved after the match.
- **Voice chat** is off until you turn it on and agree. It uses Unity Vivox, is live only, and is not recorded by
  Crony. See the [Vivox / Unity privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Reports** you make are handled by the host's game during the match. There is no central moderation server.

## What Crony does not do

- No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-game purchases.
- No collection of your real name, email, location or contacts.

## Children

Crony is intended for players aged **13 and over** because it has live text and voice chat with other players.

If the game starts collecting anything new, this page will be updated before that version is released.
